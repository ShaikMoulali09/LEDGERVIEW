About LEDGERVIEW

Built for small teams who deserve clear numbers, not a data team.

Scroll

Most small businesses already have the data they need. It's sitting in a CSV export, a billing tool, or a folder of spreadsheets nobody has opened since the quarter it was made. Ledgerview exists to close that one small, expensive gap between having the numbers and actually seeing them.

We built it after watching the same pattern play out with freelancers, small shop owners and student teams alike: real data, no time to learn a full analytics platform, and no budget for one either. So Ledgerview does one job — turn a spreadsheet into a dashboard — and tries to do it without asking anything of you first.
